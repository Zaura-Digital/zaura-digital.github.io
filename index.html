FAQ

Questions

When are the cheat sheets ready?

Every morning before the first game of the day. A new set is built for each day's slate, and members get a Discord ping when it's live.

Where do I open them?

Inside Whop, in the browser or the Whop app on your phone. Once you join, the cheat sheets show up in your Whop membership.

Are these picks guaranteed to win?

No. The cheat sheets show recent results and current lines so you can research faster. A player hitting 5 of his last 5 is a small sample, not a promise. Treat every edge as a starting point and make your own call.

Which sportsbooks do the lines come from?

Prop lines are pulled from a range of major US sportsbooks, and we show the best over price we found for each prop. Lines move, so check your own book before you bet.

How do I cancel?

Your membership is managed through Whop, and you can cancel it from your Whop account at any time.
